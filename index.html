HOW TO PLAY

    

      
Tap anywhere to swing. Every batter gets one pitch.

      
Your timing decides where on the wall the ball lands. Aim for the hits, dodge the outs.

      
Early pulls it, late pushes it the other way: a righty pulls to left field, a lefty to right. Watch which side of the plate he's standing on.

      
Whiff, foul it off, or let it go by and you're out.

      
3 outs an inning, 3 innings a game.

      
Score the target (TGT) to unlock the next level. Fall short and it's back to ROOKIE.

      
FULL or SIMPLE? FULL is real baseball: walks, steals, sac bunts, ground balls and fly balls that move runners, runners taking the extra base and plays at the plate (and the odd error). SIMPLE keeps just singles, doubles, triples, homers and plain outs, and runners move exactly as far as the hit. Same difficulty, separate high scores.

      
Replay any level you've unlocked in PRACTICE MODE. Scores there don't count.

    

    
ON THE WALL

    
123
Single, double, triple. The batter takes that base and every runner moves up the same number of bases. In FULL, runners can take an extra base (1st to 3rd, or scoring from 2nd on a single or from 1st on a double), and a runner waved home can be thrown out at the plate.

    
HR
Home run. Everybody scores. It isn't on every wall, so grab it when it shows up. With the bases loaded it's a GRAND SLAM.

    
BB
Walk. The batter takes 1st. Runners only move up if they're forced.

    
Ground ball. An out, but runners can move up or score. With a runner on 1st and fewer than 2 outs the box is striped: it's always a double play or a fielder's choice.

    
Fly ball. An out. A runner on 3rd can tag up and score (a sac fly), and a runner on 2nd can tag up to 3rd. If the throw beats him, it's a double play.

    
K
Strikeout. An out, and nobody moves.

    
SB
Steal. Your runners take off for the next base and you find out at the bag: usually a STOLEN BASE (two runners is a DOUBLE STEAL), sometimes the lead runner is CAUGHT STEALING. The batter stays up either way.

        
SAC
Sacrifice bunt. The batter is out and both runners move up a base.

    
OUT COLORS

    
Just an out.

    
A run can score on this play.

    
Striped: a ground ball here is a double play or a fielder's choice. The lead runner is out, then the batter races to 1st and you find out if he's out too.

    
Striped and lighter: the same, and a run can score.

    
With two outs, every out ends the inning, so they're all plain red.

    
TERMS

    
DOUBLE PLAY
Two outs on one ground ball, usually the runner at 2nd and the batter at 1st.

    
FIELDER'S CHOICE
The fielders get a runner out instead of the batter, who reaches 1st.

    
SAC FLY
A fly-ball out that lets the runner on 3rd tag up and score.

    
E / ERROR
A fielder boots it and the zone flips to E. It plays like a single but isn't a hit. A dropped fly ball puts the batter on 2nd if 1st is open, and with two outs it plays like a double, since everyone was running. Grounders get booted more often than fly balls, and you won't know until it happens.

    
STRIKE
Miss the pitch or let it go by and you're out. Every batter gets one pitch.

    
FOUL
Swing too early or too late and you foul it off. Here that's an out too.

    
LOB
Left on base: runners still on when the inning ends.

    
TGT
Target: the runs you need to beat the level. HI is your best score.

    
MPH
The radar gun. Heaters are fastest, then fastballs, curves and knuckleballs; the changeup is slowest.

    
HR STREAK
Hit a homer and the next wall always has an HR box. Back-to-back homers turn some out zones into hits.

    
LEVELS
ROOKIE 5 runs, VETERAN 6, ALL-STAR 8, MVP 10, LEGEND 12. Each level adds out boxes to the wall and mixes in faster pitches.
