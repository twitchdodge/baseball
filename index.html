GLOSSARY
ON THE WALL
123
Single, double, triple. The batter takes that base and every runner moves up the same number of bases.
HR
Home run. Everybody scores. With the bases loaded it's a GRAND SLAM.
BB
Walk. The batter takes 1st. Runners only move up if they're forced.
Ground ball. An out, but runners can move up or score. With a runner on 1st it can turn into a double play or a fielder's choice.
Fly ball. An out. A runner on 3rd can tag up and score (a sac fly), and a runner on 2nd can tag up to 3rd.
K
Strikeout. An out, and nobody moves.
SB
Steal. Your runners take off for the next base and you find out at the bag: usually a STOLEN BASE (two runners is a DOUBLE STEAL), sometimes the lead runner is CAUGHT STEALING. The batter stays up either way.
SAC
Sacrifice bunt. The batter is out and both runners move up a base.
OUT COLORS
Just an out.
A run can score on this play.
A double play is possible.
Both: a run can score, or it could be a double play.
With two outs, every out ends the inning, so they're all plain red.
TERMS
DOUBLE PLAY
Two outs on one ground ball, usually the runner at 2nd and the batter at 1st.
FIELDER'S CHOICE
The fielders get a runner out instead of the batter, who reaches 1st.
SAC FLY
A fly-ball out that lets the runner on 3rd tag up and score.
E / ERROR
A fielder boots it and the zone flips to E. It plays like a single but isn't a hit. A dropped fly ball puts the batter on 2nd if 1st is open, and with two outs it plays like a double, since everyone was running. Grounders get booted more often than fly balls, and you won't know until it happens.
STRIKE
Miss the pitch or let it go by and you're out. Every batter gets one pitch.
FOUL
Swing too early or too late and you foul it off. Here that's an out too.
LOB
Left on base: runners still on when the inning ends.
TGT
Target: the runs you need to beat the level. HI is your best score.
MPH
The radar gun. Heaters are fastest, then fastballs, curves and knuckleballs; the changeup is slowest.
HR STREAK
Back-to-back homers turn some out zones into hits.
LEGEND
On the last level, HR only shows up right after a hit or a walk.
